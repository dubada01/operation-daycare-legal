Privacy policy

Duba Tech Studio built Operation Daycare for adult family members and caregivers. This policy describes the information the app and family service use to provide shared drop-off monitoring.

Effective November 24, 2026

Information we collect

Account and family information

Adults can sign in with Apple or Google. We store the display name, the account identifier the sign-in provider supplies, and an email address when the provider includes one in the verified sign-in response. We also store family membership, role, and credentials that let a joined device access the correct family. Your Apple or Google password stays with the sign-in provider.

Google Sign-In

The Google Sign-In SDK may collect account information such as name, email address, phone number, and user identifier, as well as coarse location, to provide sign-in. Google says it may use an IP address to estimate a device’s general location for fraud prevention. The SDK also declares collection of user and device identifiers, usage information, and other sign-in data for analytics. See Google’s Sign-In data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Child, daycare, and schedule information

Adult organizers may provide child names, daycare names and map pins, geofence radius, time zone, weekly or rotating schedules, one-time schedule changes, and history-retention preferences. Families should provide only the information necessary to use the service.

Arrival and notification information

The family service stores reported drop-off and pickup events, whether an arrival was automatic or manual, missed-drop-off alert history, push-delivery records, device identifiers, and Apple Push Notification service tokens. Daycare arrival detection runs on the iPhone. We do not receive or store a continuous route or live location history.

Subscription and service information

Apple processes payment. We store transaction identifiers, product, environment, expiration, trial, refund, revocation, and billing-grace status so one purchase can cover the app’s family. We may retain limited application, security, and hosting logs such as request time, error details, and network address when needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

  • Link an adult’s devices to the correct family and restore access on a replacement phone.
  • Share the family’s current configuration and confirmation state with joined adults.
  • Determine whether a scheduled deadline passed without a reported arrival.
  • Send missed-drop-off notifications and provide arrival and pickup history.
  • Verify Family Plan coverage and respond to purchase, renewal, refund, and grace-period changes.
  • Prevent abuse, investigate failures, secure the service, and provide support.

We do not sell personal information, run third-party advertising, or use family data for cross-app tracking.

When information is shared

Information is visible to adults who join the same Operation Daycare family according to their role. We use Apple for identity, purchases, and push notifications, Google for optional sign-in, and Amazon Web Services for application hosting, databases, backups, and security logs. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy commitments.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the service from fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer where the recipient must continue to protect the information described here.

Retention and deletion

Arrival and pickup history follows the organizer’s selected retention period. Production application logs are generally retained for up to 30 days. Family configuration, memberships, device registrations, and entitlement records are kept while the family exists or as needed to provide the service and resolve transactions.

An organizer can remove members, change history retention, or delete the family and its stored service data. Any adult can initiate deletion of their linked account in the app. Organizers with other family members must first transfer ownership or delete the family. Limited records may be retained when legally required or necessary to resolve fraud, security, tax, or transaction disputes.

Your choices

  • Turn off location or notification permission in iPhone Settings.
  • Use manual confirmation instead of automatic geofence detection.
  • Ask the organizer to remove your family membership.
  • Delete your linked account from the app.
  • Email us to request access, correction, deletion, or help with a privacy choice.

Changing permissions can prevent automatic detection or missed-drop-off notifications. The app shows when its safety setup is incomplete.

Children and adult accounts

Operation Daycare is intended for parents, guardians, and other adult caregivers. It does not offer child accounts and is not directed to children. Adults are responsible for having authority to provide information about children and daycare arrangements.

Security and international processing

We use access controls, per-device credentials, encryption in transit, restricted secrets, and other administrative and technical safeguards. No service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be processed in the United States or another location where a service provider operates, subject to applicable safeguards.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.
